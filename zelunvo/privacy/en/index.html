Privacy Policy

How Zelunvo handles financial data, device information and advertising choices.

Last updated: 21 November 2026

English Português Français Español

At a glance

Zelunvo is designed to be offline-first: financial records are primarily stored on your device. Some optional features — such as authorized synchronization, cloud backups, asset-price lookups and advertising — may involve communication with other devices or external services.

Zelunvo does not intentionally send balances, transactions or investment portfolios in ad requests. Advertising providers may nevertheless collect technical data and identifiers as described below.

This policy describes the functions currently available. Features may vary by platform, installed version and user preferences.

1. Controller and scope

Zelunvo is provided by Paulo Jorge Simões Rodrigues (the “Controller”). For privacy-related questions, contact paulo.jorge.rodrigues@gmail.com.

This policy covers Zelunvo functionality on Android, iPhone, Windows and Web to the extent available in the installed version. Actual processing depends on the platform, enabled features and user preferences.

2. What data is processed?

  • Financial and family data: accounts, balances, transactions, categories, tags, budgets, investment positions, family members and access permissions. Most of this information is stored in the device's local database.
  • Documents provided by users: bank statements, evidence and receipt photos imported or processed by text recognition (OCR).
  • Technical and configuration information: language, settings, identifiers of authorized synchronization devices and information needed for security, continuity and recovery.
  • Advertising data: when advertising is available and enabled, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, ad interactions, performance and diagnostic information.

3. Purposes and legal basis

Financial records are used to provide the functions requested by users, including account organization, expense tracking, reports, imports and investments. Technical information supports configuration, security, synchronization and recovery. Depending on the processing activity and circumstances, providing requested features may rely on the provision of the service; consent is sought for advertising-related processing where legally required.

Financial information retained solely on a user's device is not automatically received by the Controller merely because the application is used.

4. Storage, backups and synchronization

Zelunvo prioritizes local storage. Cloud backups are optional; where the supported backup functions are used, backup data is encrypted on the device before upload, and the recovery key must be stored separately by the user. Cloud providers may process technical metadata associated with the storage service under their own policies.

Wi-Fi synchronization between authorized devices follows the permissions configured by the user and may transfer financial records that the user has chosen to share. File imports, photography and text recognition are initiated through user actions and permissions.

Optional investment-price lookups may transmit public asset identifiers or market symbols to providers such as CoinGecko or Alpha Vantage. Those providers may also receive technical information about the requests.

5. Google AdMob advertising and consent

Where available in the installed version and platform, Zelunvo may show advertisements through Google AdMob. Advertising uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK and Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy information and collect consent choices when needed.

Google and advertising technology partners identified in the applicable consent message may receive IP addresses, approximate location inferred from IP addresses, advertising or device identifiers, ad interactions, and technical or diagnostic information. Processing depends on configuration, user choices, applicable law and the providers' policies. Requests for non-personalized ads do not necessarily exclude all processing of identifiers or technical data.

Zelunvo does not intentionally add bank balances, transaction details, categories, investment positions or financial documents to ad-targeting requests. In the current development phase, banners are experimental, restricted to mobile test builds and require local enablement on an adult profile.

For further information see Google business privacy and safety, the Google Privacy Policy, and How Google uses information from partner apps and sites.

Cookies and similar technologies: this informational website does not set its own cookies or load advertising or analytics scripts. In the app, UMP and advertising services may use identifiers or local storage as permitted by the applicable settings, consent choices and law.

6. How to change or withdraw consent

In Zelunvo, go to Settings → Advertising to disable test banners and, where UMP makes this available, open advertising privacy options to review or withdraw consent choices. Disabling the local preview prevents further advertising requests from that feature, but does not automatically remove information previously processed by third parties.

If the consent form is temporarily unavailable, the app does not bypass the block. You can try again or contact the Controller. Your operating system may also provide controls for advertising identifiers and other permissions.

7. Recipients, third parties and international transfers

Financial records stored locally are not automatically sent to the Controller simply by using the app offline. Optional functions can involve authorized devices, the cloud provider selected by the user, market-price services, Google and the advertising technology partners described in UMP consent messages. These providers also have their own privacy policies. Some may process information outside the European Economic Area, subject to applicable legal safeguards.

8. Retention and security

Local data stays on the device for as long as the user keeps it. Deleting the app or its local data does not necessarily delete exported backups or information held by external providers. Retention of technical and advertising data held by those providers is governed by their own policies and legal obligations.

Zelunvo provides access controls and, in supported workflows, encrypts backups before transmission. No security measure offers absolute protection. Users should secure their devices and safeguard their recovery keys.

9. Children

Zelunvo can organize household information, but its experimental advertising requires confirmation that the active profile is at least 18 years old. This confirmation is not an independent age-verification mechanism. Any future advertising functions intended for children would require additional assessment and compliance with applicable laws and platform policies before activation.

10. Your rights

Subject to the applicable conditions under the GDPR, individuals may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability, as well as the right to withdraw consent without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. For data held exclusively on the device, some actions can be performed directly in the app or on the device. For requests to the Controller, use the contact details below.

You may also lodge a complaint with Portugal's National Data Protection Commission (CNPD) or another competent supervisory authority.

11. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when features, service providers or legal requirements change. The published version will show the most recent update date.
