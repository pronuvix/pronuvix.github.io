Frequently asked questions

Features and getting started

Do I need an internet connection?

Core financial management works without internet access. Direct synchronization uses the local network on supported platforms. Market prices, cloud backups and advertising may require internet access.

Can I manage family finances?

Zelunvo is designed to support shared financial records and configurable permissions. Availability depends on the version.

How do I start?

Create an account, enter its opening balance and reference date, then record or import transactions. Review imported records before confirming.

Can I change devices?

Use supported backup and restore functions. Keep your recovery key separately and verify the restored data before deleting the original.

Where can I download Zelunvo?

The application is in development. Public store availability has not yet been announced.

Where are my financial records stored?

On your devices. Zelunvo does not rely on a central financial database.

How does synchronization work?

Authorized devices communicate directly over the local network, where supported. Optional cloud backup is separate from synchronization.
