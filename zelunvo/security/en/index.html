Privacy and security

Your data. On your device.

Direct device-to-device synchronization

Authorized devices exchange financial records directly over a local network, where supported. No central Zelunvo financial database is required.

Optional backup is not synchronization

Cloud backup stores an encrypted recovery copy when enabled; it is separate from direct synchronization.

Protect your devices

Use device access protection and keep your recovery key in a safe place.

Local storage

Your financial records are stored on your devices. Zelunvo does not rely on a central financial database.

Encrypted backups

Optional cloud backups are designed to be encrypted before upload. Keep the recovery key separate.

Family sharing

Share only with authorized devices and accounts, where supported.

Connectivity

Advertising, market data and optional online services may require internet access.
