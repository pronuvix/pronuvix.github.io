Financial literacy

Understand spending

Track fixed and variable expenses. Compare several months to identify patterns and irregular costs.

Build a reserve

Set a goal based on essential expenses and income stability. Keep the reserve accessible.

Understand net worth

Distinguish assets from debts. Net worth equals assets minus liabilities.

Plan investments

Consider risk, time horizon, costs and diversification. Past returns do not guarantee future results.
