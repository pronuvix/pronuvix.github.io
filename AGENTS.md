# Zelunvo website — development contract

- `main` is the sole permanent branch and production source. Create short-lived `feature/*`, `docs/*` or `fix/*` branches from current main; reuse open PRs and coordinate through ROADMAP.md.
- Contents and quality owns integration; Design and experience owns visual work. Review concurrent changes and tests before integration.
- Never merge into a deployment-triggering branch or publish without explicit user approval. Keep tasks paused until authorized to resume.
- Preserve PT-PT, EN, FR and ES, local-first financial privacy and direct authorized device synchronization. Do not invent product claims or expose personal email.
- Do not force-push shared branches or delete unrecovered work. Report actual commits and validation evidence.
